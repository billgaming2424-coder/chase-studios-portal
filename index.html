Behind the Studio
Studio Lead & Creator Solo Developer

William "Bill" Chase

Chase Studios started years ago with nothing more than a shared screen and a spark of imagination between best friends. Whenever a video game gave us a custom character creator, we built our own world around an ever-growing dynasty: the Chase Family. We played as brothers Richard "Ricky" and Daniel "Danny" Chase, throwing ourselves into every role we could dream up. We were championship wrestlers in the ring, heavy hitters on the baseball diamond, gridiron football stars, dusty frontier cowboys, and daring adventurers across dozens of worlds.

Then came Uncle Bill. The moment Bill was created, something clicked. In everyday life, I am naturally quiet and fiercely introverted. But embodying Bill gave me the courage to step into the light, greet anyone with a genuine smile, and build things without the fear of holding back. He became a second part of who I am: the creative fire, the confidence, and the voice ready to connect with the world.

Chase Studios is the living legacy of that journey. It exists to capture that exact same childhood magic—the freedom to dive into any world, explore any genre, and create unforgettable adventures. Whether you are battling alongside loyal companions in browser RPGs, connecting with friends through our software, or exploring our future technology, this studio is built on a simple promise: Every Genre. No Limits. Welcome to the family.

⚔️ Player Two Forever

Dedicated with endless gratitude to "Danny Chase" — co-architect of the Chase legacy, tag-team partner, and lifelong brother in gaming. From the late nights on the couch crafting wild character rosters to conquering every ring, diamond, and frontier we ever stepped foot into, Chase Studios exists because of the adventures we started together. Thanks for always picking up the other controller.
