THE STORY SO FAR
Two wings. One kitchen. Zero peace.

For years, The Ridge and The Swaim have shared one kitchen, one walk-in fridge, and an increasingly petty rivalry over whose dining room gets the good rolls first.

Under the Dining Director, The Ridge fights for total service supremacy. But the Assistant Dining Director — everyone just calls her The Boss — has other plans for The Swaim.

Move your crew. Capture the dining halls. And whatever you do, keep your Cook safe in the kitchen — because if the other wing ever reaches your Cook, it's lights out for your whole shift.

May the best wing win.
